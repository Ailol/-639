# q'p / partikles

The existing folder names mark `q'p` as theory and `q'partikles` as a creative space for pheno, pfeno, and feno. Keep an exploratory statement here with its generator and open questions; move an implementation to the relevant `ti'empo` branch only when its inputs and behavior are specified.

| Term | Current placement | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `q'p` | theory and exploratory propositions | no automatic claim about quantum physics |
| `q'partikles` | candidate units and relations | distinguish symbolic units from measured particles |
| `feno` | hardware or inward data | source state, before outward presentation |
| `pfeno` | pfazing between usable and understanding its use | transition and interpretation |
| `pheno` | presentable data shaped into a trait | articulated output |

The [feno → pfeno → pheno transition](../../ti'empo/pℏeno(projected%20set%20of%20looks%20and%20behaviour)/FENO_PFENO_PHENO.md) now supplies the reading order for these three terms. A candidate q'partikle may appear at any stage; record what has actually been made usable and what is only proposed.

The symbolic `E/M/C` inscription belongs to [the articulated note](../../ti'empo/pℏeno(projected%20set%20of%20looks%20and%20behaviour)/t'pheno%20(.domain)(div)/ap'article(articulate%20or%20'perform%20at%20peak')/ATOMIC_INSCRIBE.md). It can inspire this branch without serving as an empirical derivation.
