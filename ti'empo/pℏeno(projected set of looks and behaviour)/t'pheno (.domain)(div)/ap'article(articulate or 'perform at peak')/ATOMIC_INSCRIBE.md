1. **Notation:** the author's symbols and visual chain.
2. **Interpretation:** what a reader or implementation proposes those symbols mean.
3. **Evidence:** observations or tests, if any, that support a physical or biological claim.

The inscription does not by itself calculate energy, mass, or consciousness. Keep the source's wording and authorship attached when adapting it.
