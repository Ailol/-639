# A quick jog through the folders (Claude's reading, not a result)

Each item is tagged: SOLID (a real, established concept), TESTABLE (could be
checked with a small experiment), or CREATIVE (metaphor or play).

## pheno / feno (phenotype)
- SOLID: A phenotype is the observable traits of an organism, produced by
  genotype plus environment.
- CREATIVE: Extending "pheno" to attention, habits and behaviour ("phasing
  fibres in and out") as a way of describing how inner state shows up outside.

## Time pressure and attention (the stress observation)
- SOLID: Yerkes-Dodson (arousal and performance follow an inverted U);
  selective attention (the brain filters most input).
- TESTABLE: Do short deadlines or timed work blocks improve focus compared to
  open-ended time? Track error rate and finished tasks for two weeks.
- Note: the lever is the deadline, not faked stress.

## compaction vs compression
- SOLID: tar bundles; zip bundles and compresses; lossless can be unpacked
  exactly; lossy (JPEG, MP3) keeps the gist.
- CREATIVE: Mapping this onto human memory and "essence."

## spatial'kernel / fibres / registers (OS and informatics)
- SOLID: A kernel is the core every process runs on; fibres are lightweight
  threads; a register holds one small value.
- CREATIVE: Using these as images for how a person works.

## "makes process re'executable back and forth"
- TESTABLE: Reproducibility. If someone else can repeat a step and get the same
  result, it is science. This is the strongest idea in the repo.

## quantum / quanta / q'partikles / E, M, C
- CREATIVE: Personal vocabulary and notation. "Quantum" has a precise meaning
  in physics, and these terms do not make physics claims. They are a poetic
  layer. E, M, C here are separate from E = mc^2.
