# Quant' recon — measured difference and boundaries of analogy

**Input:** state A, state B, observation conditions, units and reference source.
**Output:** delta, precision/uncertainty, interpretation, limitations.
**Check:** repeat measurement or inspect two preserved states, recording what cannot be measured.

Working notation: wave % partikle; solv' / resolv / яesolv; ket¹; observer(change). These are symbolic modeling operations unless specific physical equations are supplied.

Physics calibration: quark flavors **up, down, charm** are not interchangeable with electric charge. In the Standard Model up and charm carry +2/3 e; down carries -1/3 e. Flavor, charge, spin and measurement are distinct fields. Quantum-like memory metaphors are not evidence of an actual quantum computer.
