# Inscribe — preserve the literal before the interpretation

**Input:** glyph, code, phrase, drawing, chat excerpt or symbolic claim.
**Output:** exact inscription plus a separately labeled proposed reading.
**Check:** compare character-for-character against the source (including apostrophes and Unicode).

Non-interchangeable examples: Cognit / Cog'nit / 'Cognit / Cognit'; Biochain / Biochain' / Bio'chain / B'iochain; B33 / 833. Meaning cannot be reconstructed by correcting spelling.

Records distinguish ORIGINAL, USER-DEFINITION, ASSISTANT-INTERPRETATION and TESTED-INVARIANT. Preserve revisions and uncertainties.
