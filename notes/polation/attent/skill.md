# Attent' — observe before interpreting

**Input:** a record, signal, image, user statement or logged model activation.
**Output:** a labeled observation with source, time/context, observed content, interpretation, and uncertainty.
**Check:** can another observer locate the same signal without asking the author?

Procedure: capture original; separate *noticed* from *inferred*; preserve the moment; compare with later changes; allow no conclusion.

Examples: Bitspectrum pulse/watch and representations; TheBestInscriber pin → attentional drop / A3B inscription; NeuroReact visual context; Kiff event protocol.
Evidence: https://github.com/Ailol/Bitspectrum/blob/main/pulse/watch.py and https://github.com/Ailol/TheBestInscriber/blob/main/readme.md

Boundary: a visualization of attention does not directly prove consciousness or intent. Tiny reaction signals are observations to calibrate, not diagnoses.
